\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item SOCIAL ported to budget-limited MOF selection over a similarity network.
  \item Band gaps are strongly homophilous on a QMOF-scale MOFGalaxyNet graph.

  \item Gains over random search come from the chemistry-aware embedding, not the graph.
  \item Published MOFGalaxyNet statistics match a 0.7 threshold, not the stated 0.9.
\end{itemize}
