\section{Objective O3 and GP-EI configuration}
O3 is formulated as a target-value objective, $f=|\mathrm{gap}-2.0\ \mathrm{eV}|$; it replaced a window-based formulation, $f=\max(0,1.5-\mathrm{gap},\mathrm{gap}-2.5)$, which is flat inside the window and therefore could not distinguish the hits (the MOFs closest to 2.0~eV) from other in-window MOFs. The hit set is identical under both formulations.

GP-EI uses a constant $\times$ Mat\'ern-5/2 kernel with automatic relevance determination (32 length scales) plus white noise, normalized targets, hyperparameters re-optimized every ten evaluations with five optimizer restarts (the first start initialized at the previous fit), conditioning on all observations after every evaluation, and expected improvement for minimization with $\xi=0.01$ and the best observed value as incumbent; it replaced a simpler configuration with an isotropic length scale and a single optimizer start. Re-optimizing hyperparameters every ten evaluations rather than after every evaluation reduced the cost per run by more than an order of magnitude; on the same seeds, it gave equal recall from the 2\% budget upward and slightly lower recall at the two smallest budgets for O1 and O4 (Table~\ref{tab:gprefit}).

\section{Implementation details}

\begin{table}[h]
\caption{Implementation choices.}
\label{tab:impl}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.3cm}p{11.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
Item & Choice \\
\midrule
Metal descriptors & \texttt{mendeleev} database; elements with an unbound anion (Cd, Er, Gd, Hg, Ho, Mg, Mn, Np, Pu, Sm, Th, U, Zn) assigned an electron affinity of 0~eV. \\
Embedding & Fingerprint and metal blocks standardized, each scaled to unit total variance, weighted 0.7/0.3, reduced to 32 dimensions by principal component analysis. \\
Centralities & Max-normalized to $[0,1]$ so that $\alpha_tc_j$ and $\beta_tI_j$ are commensurate; betweenness from 1000 sampled sources. \\
Long-range edges & Uniformly random pairs whose endpoints lie in different Leiden communities. \\
Communities & The Leiden partition of MOFGalaxyNet($\phi^\ast$) is used in all runs, for community-aware mutation and for family coverage. \\
Isolated agents & Updated with the global-best and elite terms only. \\
Remaining budget & $B-\lfloor B/P\rfloor P$ evaluations go to the unevaluated MOFs nearest the elite (also for DE, PSO and GA). \\
Minimum budget & $\max(\lceil bN\rceil,40)$ evaluations for budget fraction $b$; this raises the 0.5\% budget of O4 to 40 evaluations (0.75\% of its pool). \\
Greedy walk & Random unevaluated neighbor of the best evaluated MOF that still has one; random restart otherwise. \\
Ensemble TS & Ten bootstrap random forests (100 trees); at each step one member is drawn and fitted on its own bootstrap resample. \\
GA & Binary tournament selection, uniform crossover, Gaussian mutation (per-gene probability $1/D$, $\sigma=0.1$ of the range), one elite. \\
Bridge analysis & Event: first hit found in a Leiden community during a run; base rate over all \gs{} update evaluations; one-sided binomial test. \\
Statistics & Wilcoxon tests on paired differences rounded to $10^{-10}$ so that tied recall values (multiples of $1/K$) are ranked identically. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
